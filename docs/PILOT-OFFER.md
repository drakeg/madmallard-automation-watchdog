# Five-Job Automation Watchdog Setup — Pilot Offer

**Proposed pilot price: $399 one-time.** This is a pricing hypothesis, not a validated market rate.

## The problem

Scheduled inventory imports, customer syncs, and recurring reports may fail silently. The server remains online, while the business result is missing or stale.

## What the pilot delivers

For **up to five existing scheduled jobs on one customer-owned Linux/Docker environment**, configure expected completion windows, job-specific failure signals and one agreed output-validity check per job. Demonstrate synthetic missed-run and failure alerts, recovery notifications, and a basic incident evidence export. Deliver operator access instructions, an escalation checklist, and **seven days of configuration corrections**.

## Customer prerequisites

An authorized Linux/Docker environment, access to existing job operators, a supported HTTPS notification webhook, a safe output folder for read-only checks, and a named incident owner. Production requires secure TLS and appropriately independent monitoring infrastructure. Hosting and notification delivery costs are customer responsibilities.

## Boundaries

The offer excludes repair of broken jobs, ongoing hosting, 24/7 monitoring response, guaranteed incident prevention, production infrastructure provisioning, paid APIs or messaging, and work beyond five jobs. Any additional implementation or repair is separately scoped and quoted. Preserve customer data and never transmit records in heartbeat payloads.

## Proof of completion

Customer sees an agreed synthetic job fail, receives a notification, sees an output-invalid alert, reviews the dashboard, validates a recovery and exports redacted incident evidence. A job exiting successfully is not alone proof that its business output is correct.
