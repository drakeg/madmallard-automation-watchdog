## Qualification decisions

- **Technical fit:** one environment, five jobs or fewer, explicit owner, monitorable outputs.
- **Pain confirmed:** a meaningful recent missed result or a credible unmonitored failure path.
- **Go threshold:** at least two qualified prospects confirm an important unmonitored job, with one written pilot acceptance.
- **Revise:** existing checks cover both execution and output correctness, or implementation cannot fit reasonable delivery effort.
- **Unknown:** no response or no evidence does not equal rejection or validation.

Do not send messages or scrape personal contact details automatically. Record only evidence you actually obtained.
